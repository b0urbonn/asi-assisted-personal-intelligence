# Vision & Goals

## What is ASI?

**Assisted Personal Intelligence (ASI)** is a cross-platform AI companion — on your phone, your desktop, and the web — that acts as a persistent, context-aware layer over your digital life.

Unlike a chatbot that resets every session, ASI **accumulates context**: it knows your routines, remembers your goals, understands your schedule, and works quietly in the background so you don't have to.

## The Problem

Your life is scattered across tools that don't talk to each other:

- 📱 Phone has reminders, messages, and quick capture
- 💻 Desktop has deep work, documents, and meetings
- 🗓️ Calendar knows *when*, task apps know *what*, email knows *who*
- 🧠 Your head knows *why* — and it's overloaded

You become the integration layer. Things slip. You context-switch constantly. The tools serve the workflow instead of the other way around.

## The Vision

ASI becomes the single intelligent layer across all your devices:

| Capability | What it means |
|------------|---------------|
| **Remembers** | Preferences, routines, recurring commitments, goals — persistent across devices |
| **Understands** | Context across tasks, calendar, email, and notes |
| **Anticipates** | Morning briefings, smart suggestions, follow-up nudges |
| **Acts** | Drafts replies, schedules reminders, organizes — always with your approval |
| **Follows you** | Same assistant, same memory, same context — phone ↔ desktop ↔ web |

## Design Principles

1. **Privacy first** — your data stays yours. Local-first where possible; transparent about what's stored and why.
2. **Assist, don't replace** — ASI augments your judgment; never acts without your approval.
3. **Calm technology** — proactive but quiet. No notification noise; only what matters, when it matters.
4. **One mind, every device** — seamless sync is a core feature, not an afterthought.
5. **Open and extensible** — integrations and plugins so ASI grows with you.

## Success Looks Like

- 🌅 You open ASI on your phone each morning → a 30-second briefing: today's schedule, top tasks, anything needing attention.
- 💻 At your desk, ASI on desktop picks up exactly where your phone left off — same conversation, same context.
- 🔔 Reminders arrive at the right time, in the right context — not as noise.
- 📊 Weekly reviews happen automatically with insights into where your time went.
- 🤝 You trust ASI enough to let it handle routine follow-ups.

## Non-Goals (for now)

- ASI is **not** a social network or content platform.
- ASI is **not** trying to replace your judgment or decision-making.
- ASI is **not** a general-purpose AI playground — it's focused on personal organization and life management.
