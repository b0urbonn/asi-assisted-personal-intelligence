# Vision & Goals

## What is ASI?

**Assisted Personal Intelligence (ASI)** is a personal AI assistant that goes beyond answering questions. It is designed to be a persistent, context-aware companion that understands your routines, remembers what matters to you, and proactively helps you stay organized.

## The Problem

Most productivity tools are passive:

- Task managers require you to manually enter and update everything.
- Chatbots forget context between sessions.
- Calendar and email tools don't talk to each other.
- Notifications are noisy and rarely helpful.

The result: you become the integration layer between your tools, and important things slip through the cracks.

## The Vision

ASI will be the layer that connects your digital life:

- **Remembers** — your preferences, routines, recurring commitments, and goals.
- **Understands** — context across tasks, calendar, email, and notes.
- **Anticipates** — surfaces what you need before you ask.
- **Acts** — drafts replies, schedules reminders, and keeps things moving.

## Design Principles

1. **Privacy first** — your personal data stays yours. Local-first where possible, transparent about what is stored and why.
2. **Assist, don't replace** — ASI augments your judgment; it never acts on your behalf without your approval.
3. **Simple by default** — powerful underneath, effortless to use.
4. **Open and extensible** — integrations and plugins so ASI grows with you.

## Success Looks Like

- You open ASI each morning and get a brief: today's schedule, pending tasks, and anything needing attention.
- Reminders arrive at the right time, in the right context — not as noise.
- Weekly reviews happen automatically, with insights into where your time went.
- You trust ASI enough to let it handle routine follow-ups.
